In our previous discussion on oil parameters, we highlighted that **HTHS dictates the tradeoff between fuel economy and high-RPM protection**, and that **VI (Viscosity Index) alone is not a reliable quality indicator**.

Many car owners still wonder: why do two bottles labeled **`5W-30`** drive completely differently? And why do some hyped oils feel buttery smooth for the first $$2000$$ km, only to become noisy and sluggish after $$3000$$ km? The answers lie in two key metrics: **HTHS** and **VII**.

## 1. HTHS Viscosity: The Real Metric of Oil Film Strength

The `5W-30` rating on the bottle only describes kinematic viscosity measured at $$100^\circ\text{C}$$. But under intense driving, temperatures between the piston ring and cylinder wall spike to **$$150^\circ\text{C}$$**, where the lubricant experiences millions of shear cycles per second.

**HTHS (High Temperature High Shear)** measures true dynamic oil film thickness at **$$150^\circ\text{C}$$ under extreme shear stress** (expressed in $$\text{mPa}\cdot\text{s}$$ or $$\text{cP}$$). This single parameter divides `5W-30` into two distinct categories:

| 5W-30 Category | HTHS Specification | Typical Approvals | Characteristics & Best Fit |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Low HTHS**<br>**(Thin Film・Fuel Economy)** | **$$2.9 \sim 3.1$$** | API SP / SQ, ILSAC GF-6 / GF-7<br>ACEA A5/B5, **ACEA C2** | Responsive throttle, free-revving, highest fuel economy.<br>Ideal for **Asian, American, and Hybrid vehicles**. |
| **High HTHS**<br>**(Thick Film・Protection)** | **$$\ge 3.5$$** | ACEA A3/B4, **ACEA C3**<br>MB 229.5 / 229.51 / 229.52<br>BMW LL-01 / LL-04, VW 504/507 | Tough hydrodynamic cushion, superior wear defense.<br>Essential for **European turbo, diesel, and performance engines**. |

> **Important Application Rule**:
> Putting an `ACEA C3` oil ($$\text{HTHS} \ge 3.5$$) into an Asian naturally aspirated commuter car won't cause damage, but you will notice **heavier throttle response and higher fuel consumption**. Conversely, running an `API SP / ILSAC GF-6` oil ($$\text{HTHS} \approx 2.9$$) in a European turbo engine requiring $$\text{HTHS} \ge 3.5$$ will lead to accelerated **camshaft and cylinder wall wear** under sustained boost!

## 2. VII (Viscosity Index Improvers): Why Oils Degrade at 3,000 km

To formulate multigrade oils like `5W-30` or `0W-40`, blenders start with a **thin base oil** (to meet cold-cranking specs) and blend in **VII polymers** (coiled molecular spring chains):
- **Cold temperatures**: Polymer chains curl into tight coils, allowing smooth cold flow.
- **High temperatures**: The chains expand and interlock like a mesh, artificially thickening the thin base oil to achieve a 30 or 40 grade.

### What Is "Mechanical Shear Down"?

As engine oil circulates through **timing chains, oil pump gears, camshafts, and piston rings**, these expanded polymer chains get physically sliced apart like scissors cutting string!

- **Budget / Inflated Oils (Inferior base oil + excess cheap VII)**: To achieve inflated VI figures ($$\text{VI} > 190$$), blenders load cheap linear polymers. The oil starts quiet, but within $$3000\sim 5000$$ km the polymer chains are sheared to pieces. The oil collapses back into thin base oil, causing **drastic drops in HTHS and viscosity**—the real culprit behind engine harshness.
- **OEM-Approved Synthetic Oils (PAO/GTL base + star polymers)**: Premium certified oils rely on high-grade **PAO / GTL synthetic bases** that naturally resist thinning, minimizing VII dependency, and utilize resilient "Star Polymers" that maintain viscosity integrity beyond $$10000$$ km.

## 3. Three Practical Rules for Choosing Engine Oil

1. **Identify Your Vehicle's "HTHS Camp" First**:
   - **Asian/US/Hybrid cars**: Choose low-drag fuel economy oils matching `API SQ / SP`, `ILSAC GF-7 / GF-6`, or `ACEA C2` ($$\text{HTHS} \approx 2.9$$).
   - **European Turbo/Diesel engines**: Mandate robust protection matching `ACEA C3` or `A3/B4` ($$\text{HTHS} \ge 3.5$$).
2. **Insist on Genuine "OEM Approvals" to Prevent Shear Breakdown**:
   Oils officially approved under standards like `MB 229.5 / 229.51 / 229.52`, `VW 504/507`, `BMW LL-04`, or `Porsche A40` must pass brutal **high-pressure injector Stay-in-Grade shear tests**, ensuring consistent protection throughout the service interval.
3. **Do Not Chase Unrealistically High "Viscosity Index (VI)"**:
   Standard high-quality `5W-30` oils feature VI values between $$160 \sim 175$$. Any uncertified brand advertising $$\text{VI} > 190$$ or $$200+$$ typically relies on volatile thickeners that break down rapidly.
